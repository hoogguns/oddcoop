# OddCoop

**OddCoop** is a cooperative device buyback and same-day trade-in network.

We help people and partners turn used phones, tablets, and other devices into **fast, fair cash** — with transparent pricing, local fulfillment where possible, and tools that put independent shops and drivers on the same network instead of locking value inside a single brand or warehouse.

## Mission

- **Same-day outcomes** — quotes, trade-ins, and payments that move at the speed of real life, not multi-week mail-in cycles.
- **Fair, competitive offers** — pricing informed by live market data so customers and partners are not left guessing.
- **Cooperative model** — partners (repair shops, retailers, drivers) share infrastructure, inventory routes, and demand instead of competing alone against national giants.
- **One product, dual brand** — the same platform powers **Buyback Network** ([buybacknetwork.com](https://buybacknetwork.com)) and **Odd Coop** ([oddcoop.com](https://oddcoop.com)); brand resolves from the request host.
- **Trust and security** — secure sessions, careful handling of customer and partner data, and continuous hardening of the code that quotes devices, runs partner dashboards, and moves money-adjacent workflows.
